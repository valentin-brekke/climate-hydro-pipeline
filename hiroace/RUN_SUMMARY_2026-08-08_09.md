## 5. Comparison to the HiRO-ACE paper (arXiv [2512.18224](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.18224))

The paper reports throughput on a single **NVIDIA H100** (Isambard-AI's GH200 pairs a comparable Hopper-class GPU with a Grace CPU, so this is a like-for-like GPU comparison):

| Metric | Paper (1× H100) | This run (GH200) | Delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| ACE2S throughput | 1,500 simulated years/day (1°×1°) → 19.2 min for 20 sim-years | 23.8 min for 20 sim-years (2 members × 10 yr) | **~24% slower** (checkpoint loading, container/I/O overhead, ~15 GB Zarr writes) |
| HiRO downscaling | ~45 min per 16°×16° patch-year (1 GPU) | ~48.9 min per patch-year, single-GPU-equivalent (GPU-hours ÷ member-years, 16°×16° Japan patch) | **~9% slower** |
| HiRO practical ensemble throughput | 45 min → 1 patch-year, ensemble size unspecified | 45 min → **wall-clock isn't 1:1 with paper's single-run figure**, but ~49 min of wall-clock (4 GPUs parallel) yields a full **4-member ensemble-year**, since ensemble members are batched together per time-chunk rather than costing proportionally more GPU time | Effectively ~4× ensemble throughput for near-identical wall time, at the cost of 4× GPU allocation |
| Training compute (context only, not reproduced this run) | ACE2S: 7 days / HiRO: 4 days, both on 8× H100 | — | n/a (inference-only runs) |
| Reference: X-SHiELD source simulation (context) | 0.12 simulated years/day on 27,648 AMD EPYC 9654 cores; 74.6 GJ/simulated year | — | ACE2S is ~**12,500×** faster throughput than the physics-based simulation it emulates |

**Takeaway:** both stages ran within ~10–25% of the paper's published single-GPU benchmarks — well within expected overhead for containerized SLURM jobs (image load, checkpoint I/O, Zarr writes) — confirming the pipeline is performing as expected on Isambard-AI GH200 hardware. The main practical difference from the paper's per-GPU figures is that this weekend's HiRO job used 4 GPUs to parallelize across *time*, generating the full 4-member ensemble in about the same wall-clock time the paper quotes for a single deterministic patch-year.
